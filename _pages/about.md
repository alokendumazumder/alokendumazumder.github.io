<p>I am a Senior Researcher at the Security Science (SS) Laboratory of Fujitsu Research of India. Previously, I was a Prime Minister&#x27;s Research Fellow (PMRF) PhD at the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru,
        advised by <a href="https://www.vistalabiisc.com/P_portfolio/profile" target="_blank">Prof. Punit Rathore</a>.
        My doctoral research lies at the intersection of <strong>stochastic optimization theory</strong> and
        <strong>deep representation learning</strong>.</p>

<p>I have had the privilege of conducting research at MIT&#x27;s Senseable City Lab, Dolby Laboratories
        (Advanced Technology Group), and IBM Research — working on problems ranging from
        diffusion bridge models for image restoration to context-length bias in time-series forecasting.</p>

<p>My broader interests span optimization theory, deep learning, computer vision, and stochastic modelling,
        with a focus on principled, theoretically grounded approaches to modern learning problems.</p>
